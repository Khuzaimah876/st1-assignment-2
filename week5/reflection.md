Requirements must be supported by evidence (stakeholder or clients statement, example: system creates record)  because the system should be built from the stakeholders needs rather than assumptions by the developing team. The evidence ensures that the requirements are found and match the stakeholders expectations ensuring they are not fabricated. Without evidence, the developing team can add unnecessary features or make decisions that don't align with the stakeholder or clients expectation. That is why the requirements should not be confusing so they don't allude to another meaning causing problems down the line.
